Electrolyte additives and cycle life in LFP cells

Cells were cycled at 1C between 2.5 and 3.65 V at 25 °C, twelve cells per batch. Both batches started at full capacity. After 500 cycles, capacity retention rose from 78% to 91% with the additive, and the gap opened after cycle 200 (Fig. 3, Table S2).

The open context layer for reasoning agents.

We started OpenGraph AI because retrieval-augmented agents are stuck on text. Real problems live across tables, slides, recordings, and screenshots — and the answers live in the relationships between them. We're building a single, open graph layer so agents can reason across all of it.

Open by design.

One context layer, designed to fit the stack you already trust.

  • Yours to run. Apache-2.0 packages that run on your machine. The graph is a local JSON file you own.
  • Connect every signal. Images, documents, and tables share one node, edge, and evidence model today. Audio and video are next.
  • Fits your stack. graph.json loads straight into NetworkX, so it drops into the pipelines and notebooks you already run.
  • One interface for context. Give agents relationships and evidence through one tool—not another service they need to supervise.
